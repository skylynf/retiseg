How to read

Each image has three kinds of step.

  1. Image assessment. Grade the photograph with no outlines shown.
  2. Marked regions. For each dashed circle, first say what you see (A). Then reveal the outlines of the dataset annotation and the model, and explain the difference (B). Your answer to A is locked once you reveal.
  3. Image summary. All outlines are shown. Judge whether the model's lesions alone would change the grade or the referral.
