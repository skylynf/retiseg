\section{Supplementary information}
\noindent\textbf{Supplementary tables} (separate document, generated from the same data table).
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,label=\textbf{\arabic*}.,itemsep=1pt]
\item Literature audit: the setup stated by each method paper (split, resolution, AUPR implementation, baseline provenance).
\item One method, many reported values: every method reported in at least two papers on IDRiD or DDR, with the spread and the provenance of the values.
\item Training amount, batch and selection of every main-table run.
\item Per-class AUPR with 95\% bootstrap intervals of every main-table model on every test set, both sources.
\item Secondary metrics on the internal tests: 11-threshold AUPR, Dice, IoU, lesion-level precision and recall by lesion size, image-level AUC.
\item Registered and revised recipes of the three models whose per-image loss term collapsed on rare classes.
\item Every factor cell, per class and per test set.
\item Computational cost.
\item Refined IDRiD: every IDRiD-trained model against the official and the refined labels, and the label differences.
\item Encoder comparison on both training sources.
\item Ensembles of every registered composition on every test set, and the reference model.
\item Results by DR grade.
\item Human reference on DiaRetDB1.
\item Annotation style of every source: components per image and their size.
\item Near-duplicate audit and the images excluded.
\item Released checkpoints on every test set, per class.
\item Foundation models without training on every test set.
\item Experiment names used in the text and the identifiers used in the repository.
\end{enumerate}
\noindent\textbf{Supplementary notes.} Note 1, one entry per registered model variant (code used, reproducibility level, changes); Note 2, the training budget, the selection rule, the disclosed revisions and the scope changes; Note 3, the evaluation protocol. \textbf{Supplementary data.} Split lists and exclusions; the literature extraction; the results table with every run's recipe and environment record; probability maps where the licence allows.
